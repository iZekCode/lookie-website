## Frequently Asked Questions

**The camera isn’t detecting my child’s hand.** Make sure the room is well-lit and the iPad is placed on a stable surface. Position your child so their hands and arms are clearly visible on screen. Then, make a fist to start painting. Also, check that Lookie has permission to access the camera in iPad Settings.

**How do I delete my account and its data?** Open the Profile page in the app and select Delete Account. Your account, along with all paintings, statistics, and templates stored on our servers, will be deleted. If you’re using Guest Mode, deleting the app from your iPad will remove the data stored on that device. You can also email us if you need help with the process.

**Why did my paintings disappear after signing out?** When you sign out, paintings stored on that iPad are removed once the backup is complete. This helps prevent your paintings from appearing in another account when sharing an iPad. To get your paintings back, simply sign in with the same Apple ID.

**Why can’t I create a custom template?** The template creation feature requires an account signed in with Apple and an active internet connection. Each account has a daily image creation limit. Once you’ve reached it, you can try again the next day.

**What does the Movement Report do?** The Movement Report helps parents review their child’s hand activity, range of motion, play time, and stroke accuracy. It provides a general overview of their child’s activity while using Lookie. All measurements are camera-based estimates, not medical assessments or diagnoses.
