## Frequently asked questions

**The camera does not recognize my child's hand.**
Make sure the room is bright and the iPad stands steady. Stand until the hand and arm are visible on screen, then make a fist to start painting. Also check that camera access for Lookie is on in the iPad Settings.

**How do I delete the account and its data?**
Open the profile page in the app and choose delete account. The account and all paintings, stats and templates on our server are deleted too. Without an account (guest mode), deleting the app from the iPad deletes everything. You can also email the address above.

**Paintings disappeared after signing out.**
When you sign out, the paintings on that iPad are removed once the backup is done, so a shared iPad does not copy them into another account. Sign in again with the same Apple ID to get them back.

**"Buat Sendiri" does not work.**
This feature needs an account (Sign in with Apple) and an internet connection, and is limited to 2 pictures a day per account. Try again tomorrow if the limit is used up.

**What is the movement report for?**
The report helps parents see hand activity, range of motion, play time and line accuracy. The numbers are camera estimates and not a diagnostic tool.
